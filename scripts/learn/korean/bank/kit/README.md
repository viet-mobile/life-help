# Korean practice bank: localization kits

`<locale>.json` holds the user-language wording of the bank: form templates (prompt / hints / explanation), the English-meaning texts `g:<id>`, situations `s:<id>`, grammar notes `n:<skill>:<key>`, and the K7 bridge titles. The Korean target text is never translated. The canonical English templates live in `lib/learn/content/bank/korean-kit.en.json`; `source.en.json` / `source.minimal.en.json` are generated by `build.mjs` and are the translators' source.

| Tier | Locales | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (minimal) | the other 32 locales | templates + bridge strings + the 40 texts the K7 bridge lessons use |
| 2 (full) | ko, vi, ja, zh-Hans, zh-Hant, th, id, uz, mn, ru, es, fr, de, ne | every text of the bank |

Validate: `node scripts/learn/korean/bank/validate-kit.mjs <locale> <file> [--minimal]`.
All kits are AI translations that have not had a native or teacher review. The bank stays DRAFT; before publishing, every locale must be complete (`build-content-assets.mjs` enforces this for a PUBLISHED bank).
